ABOUT THE UNIVERSITY

Discover Assam University

Assam University came into existence in 1994 through the enforcement of the Assam (Central) University Act 1989. Nestled among the scenic, sprawling green hillocks of Dargakona (600 acres) near Silchar, and complemented by the picturesque Diphu Campus (90 acres) in Karbi Anglong, the University serves as an academic beacon for Northeast India.

16 Schools & 42 Departments

Comprehensive multidisciplinary learning across science, technology, management, arts, and humanities.

690+ Acres Green Campus

Pristine biodiversity campuses in Silchar (Dargakona) and Karbi Anglong (Diphu) fostering sustainable education.

NAAC 'A' Grade Institution

Central University recognized for research publications, state-of-the-art laboratories, and regional development.
